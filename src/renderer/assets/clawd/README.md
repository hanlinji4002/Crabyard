Clawd sprites for the Clawd tub (`src/renderer/components/clawd-tank.ts`), taken
from [clawd-tank](https://github.com/marciogranzotto/clawd-tank)
(`assets/svg-animations/`, MIT — see `LICENSE`). Renamed after the animation
they play; `happy.svg` and `sleeping.svg` have their viewBox widened to the
shared `-15 -25 45 45` so every sprite draws Clawd at the same place and scale.
